Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Overdrive Music Studio (ABN 47 193 365 574) is based in Toowoomba, Queensland. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, what we do with it, and how to contact us about it. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, to the extent they apply to us.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, your phone number or email, and details about your event (type, date, guest numbers, venue and anything you add). You choose what to put in. If you phone or email us instead, we keep what you tell us in the same way.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan and run your event if you book us.
  • To keep the business records we are required to keep, such as for tax.

We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list. We will only send you information about your own enquiry or booking.

How the form works

When you press “Send now”, your enquiry is sent through Web3Forms, a form service, which emails it to us. Web3Forms may process it on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. Once it reaches us, it is kept in our email inbox.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It does load its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request to Google, which can see your IP address and browser details. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs of visits.

Links to other sites

Our Facebook and Instagram links take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by us.

Keeping your information

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to quote, run your event and meet our record-keeping duties, then delete them. We keep email secure with a password and do not share your details except as described above or where the law requires it.

Access, corrections and complaints

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing overdrivestudios21@gmail.com or calling 0418 587 668. If you have a complaint about how we handled your information, contact us first and we will respond. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
